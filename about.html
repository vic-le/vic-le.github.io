Vic Le / The Living Archive

I'm Vic Le, an emerging Library and Information Science professional interested in archives, digital collections, information organization, and cultural preservation.

My background in administrative and records-oriented work has informed my appreciation for accuracy, accessible information, and thoughtful documentation. Through LIS coursework, I am developing skills in information behavior research, database design, metadata, and indexing.

Photography and visual storytelling are complementary interests that shape how I think about the preservation and interpretation of personal and community histories.

Areas of Interest

Archives and special collections · Digital collections · Metadata and description · Information behavior · Cultural heritage · Community-centered access
