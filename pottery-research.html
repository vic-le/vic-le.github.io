LIS Project / Information Behavior

Information-Seeking & Pottery

Investigating how intermediate pottery practitioners find, evaluate, exchange, and apply information.

Project Overview

This LIS coursework explores the information needs and practices of intermediate pottery communities. It considers the role of studios, peers, online resources, and experience in shaping creative decisions.

Theoretical Lens

Marcia Bates' Berrypicking Model provides a lens for examining information seeking as an evolving, iterative process rather than a single fixed search.

Skills Demonstrated

Information behavior research · Literature review · Research synthesis · Community analysis · Academic writing
