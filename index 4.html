The person behind the archive

Curiosity is a form of preservation.

I'm interested in the ways photography, archives, and storytelling preserve the histories embedded in everyday life. My creative practice explores the connections between people, places, objects, and cultural memory.

Through visual documentation and research, I hope to highlight stories that deserve to be remembered—creating thoughtful collections that connect personal experiences with broader community histories.

“An archive begins with the decision that something is worth remembering.”
